How much will my treatment cost?

We give you a clear price after your examination, before any treatment starts. Implants start from ฿40,000 and ceramic or zirconia crowns from ฿15,000.

Are you open at weekends?

Yes — we’re open every day, including Saturday and Sunday, until 20:00.

How do I get there?

From BTS On Nut, walk into Century the Movie Plaza 2 and take the escalator to the 3rd floor.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Tell us when you book or arrive. We’ll explain each step before we start and go at a pace that feels right for you.

Can I come in today?

Call 064 196 5596 and we’ll check for a free slot. If you’re in pain, tell us — we’ll do our best to see you soon.
