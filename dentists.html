Our dentists · ทันตแพทย์เฉพาะทาง

Treated by the right specialist, every time.

Bangkok Dental Care is a dental specialist clinic. Each case is matched to the dentist trained for it — so treatment is planned properly, done gently and followed up by the same hands.

Specialties · สาขาเฉพาะทาง

Five areas of care, one team.

  • 01

    จัดฟัน

    Orthodontics

    Metal braces, ceramic braces and clear aligners — from first assessment to retainers.

  • 02

    ศัลยกรรมช่องปาก

    Implants & oral surgery

    Implant placement, extractions and wisdom teeth, planned with panoramic X-ray.

  • 03

    ทันตกรรมประดิษฐ์

    Prosthodontics

    Crowns, bridges, veneers and dentures that restore function and appearance.

  • 04

    รักษารากฟัน

    Endodontics

    Root canal treatment to relieve pain and keep your natural tooth.

  • 05

    ทันตกรรมทั่วไป

    General & cosmetic

    Check-ups, scaling, fillings and whitening for everyday dental health.
