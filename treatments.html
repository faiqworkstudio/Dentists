Treatments · บริการของเรา

Complete dental care, one clinic.

Nine treatments, from routine cleaning to implants — each planned with an examination, an X-ray where needed, and a price agreed before we begin.

Dental implants

A titanium root and a natural-looking crown replace a missing tooth for good. Placement is planned with the on-site panoramic X-ray, and you see the full cost before surgery.

from ฿40,000

02

จัดฟัน

Braces & clear aligners

Straighten teeth with metal braces, ceramic braces or near-invisible clear aligners. Regular adjustment visits keep treatment on track — and patients often mention the fair price.

Consultation first

03

ครอบฟันสีขาว

Ceramic & zirconia crowns

Tooth-coloured crowns protect cracked, worn or root-treated teeth and restore their shape — matched to the shade of your smile.

from ฿15,000

04

รักษารากฟัน

Root canal treatment

Removes infection from inside the tooth to stop the pain and save your natural tooth, usually finished with a crown.

05

วีเนียร์

Veneers

Thin, tooth-coloured shells bonded to the front of teeth to change their colour, shape or spacing.

06

ฟอกสีฟัน

Teeth whitening

In-clinic whitening that lifts stains safely, supervised by a dentist from start to finish.

07

อุดฟัน

Tooth-coloured fillings

Composite fillings for cavities and chips that blend with your teeth — with a follow-up if anything feels off.

08

ขูดหินปูน

Scaling & cleaning

A thorough clean above and below the gumline to remove tartar and keep gums healthy. Recommended every 6–12 months.

09

ฟันปลอม

Dentures

Removable partial or full dentures, made to fit comfortably and look natural.

Prices are starting prices and depend on your case; the clinic confirms the final cost after examination.
