Treatments

Complete care, explained simply.

Nine treatments, from a routine clean to implants. Whatever you need, we start with a proper look, explain your options in plain words and agree the price before we begin.

Dental implants Braces & clear aligners Ceramic & zirconia crowns Root canal treatment Veneers Teeth whitening Tooth-coloured fillings Scaling & cleaning Dentures
01

Dental implants

A missing tooth replaced for good, with a titanium root and a natural-looking crown. We plan it with the on-site panoramic X-ray and talk you through every step — and the full cost — before surgery.

from ฿40,000

02

Braces & clear aligners

Metal braces, ceramic braces or near-invisible clear aligners. We’ll help you choose what suits your teeth, your lifestyle and your budget, then see you regularly to keep things on track.

Consultation first

03

Ceramic & zirconia crowns

Tooth-coloured crowns that protect cracked, worn or root-treated teeth and bring back their shape — matched to the shade of your smile.

from ฿15,000

04

Root canal treatment

Clears infection from inside the tooth to stop the pain and save your natural tooth. Usually finished with a crown.

05

Veneers

Thin, tooth-coloured shells bonded to the front of teeth to refresh their colour, shape or spacing.

06

Teeth whitening

In-clinic whitening that lifts stains safely, with a dentist looking after you from start to finish.

07

Tooth-coloured fillings

Composite fillings for cavities and chips that blend in with your teeth. If anything feels off afterwards, come back and we’ll adjust it.

08

Scaling & cleaning

A thorough clean above and below the gumline to remove tartar and keep gums healthy. We recommend one every 6–12 months.

09

Dentures

Partial or full dentures, made to fit comfortably and look natural.

Prices are starting prices and depend on your case — we confirm the final cost with you after your examination.
